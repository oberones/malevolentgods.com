Explore Enoch Root’s art as a collection of living, interactive compositions. Prismatic Orrery sets colorful geometric kites turning in opposing directions, Multicolored Ocean sends tides of pigment across a painted surface, and Sacred Geometry brings a metallic mandala to life with breathing layers and traveling reflections.

Shape each artwork with its interactive controls, adjust the motion, and zoom in to explore the details. Try different palettes in Prismatic Orrery and Multicolored Ocean, slow a loop down, or pause and scrub the timeline to find a favorite moment. Save an image as a PNG, or reset the artwork to return to its original composition.

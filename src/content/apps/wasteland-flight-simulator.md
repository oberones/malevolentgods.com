Wasteland Flight Simulator puts a makeshift glider over a streamed post-apocalyptic landscape of mountains, cracks, ruined towers, fog, and ash. Fly with keyboard, pointer, or touch controls; manage throttle, bank through turns, recover from stalls, and try not to make the terrain part of the cockpit.

Underneath the dust are deterministic fixed-step physics, adaptive visual quality, and a single-page WebGL runtime. It works on current desktop and mobile browsers, with landscape-only controls on phones and tablets. There are no accounts, saves, or telemetry. The wasteland is not taking attendance.

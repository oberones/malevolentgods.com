Virtual Soundforge is a musical sketchpad for discovering melodies and harmonies through experimentation. Choose a key, tempo, and sound, then start a loop and follow your ears. Explore fresh combinations with Random mode, build repeating motifs with Phrase, or create arrangements with distinct sections in Song mode.

Layer up to six voices, move between warm, glassy, organ, dark, and lush sounds, and shape how busy, varied, or dramatic the music becomes. Adjust the controls while it plays to reshape upcoming notes, with an animated visualizer responding alongside the sound. Shuffle the seed for a new idea, or reuse the same seed and settings to recreate a favorite.

When something clicks, export the current composition as a WAV file or take its notes into another music application with MIDI. Everything runs in your browser, with no account required.

Markov Playground is a musical toy for following your curiosity. Start with a preset and listen as notes, chords, and silences take an ever-changing path through a glowing constellation. Adjust the speed, sound, and Surprise, or select a sound and change the chances of what comes next.

Bring your own MIDI or MusicXML excerpt to explore new possibilities, with files processed on your device. Freeze a favorite phrase to hear it repeat, then export it as WAV audio or MIDI notes. Save your toy in the browser or download a portable copy to revisit its sounds and settings another day. No account or musical knowledge is needed.

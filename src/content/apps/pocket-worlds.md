Pocket Worlds puts miniature 3D scenes in your hands, with interactive controls that let you change the conditions and see how each little world responds.

Start with a ship in a bottle: adjust the wind and wave strength, or shake the bottle to send the water and ship sloshing around. A quiet nautical keepsake is only a few bad decisions away from a very small storm.

At Veyr Quay, each receding tide reveals another stair into the Drowned Observatory. Play a relic seeker in this browser roguelite of coastal cosmic horror: explore impossible chambers, open salvage coffers, and face the Threshold Keepers guarding the way deeper. Every descent brings randomized encounters and discoveries.

Shape your build with stat allocation, a passive skill, and up to six equipped relics. Combat unfolds automatically, so your preparation determines how you fare against the creatures below the breakwater. Choose upgrades as your Attunement grows, spend Quay Marks on Tide Offerings for bonuses, or accept a Deepening Curse for stronger enemies and better loot.

Death or abandoning an expedition resets your run, but your recovered relics, inventory, and Quay Marks remain for the next descent. Progress saves locally in your browser, so you can return to an unfinished expedition. The Expedition Journal also lets you export your character and import it elsewhere, bringing your holdings into a fresh run.
